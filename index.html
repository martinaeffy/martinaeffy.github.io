About Me

Hi everybody! My name is Martina, and I'm originally from Italy. I'm currently based in Amsterdam, where I graduated from the Royal Conservatoire of The Hague, specializing in jazz singing with a minor in jazz guitar. My passion for music began when my mum gifted me a guitar at the age of six. From that moment, I felt a deep connection to music that has stayed with me ever since. I started with classical guitar, and later discovered my love for singing too. I truly enjoyed spending hours singing, playing guitar, learning new songs, and constantly challenging myself to improve. Today, I feel excited to share my knowledge and help others discover their own way of expressing themselves through music. My goal is to make your musical journey enjoyable, motivating, and inspiring, while offering all the support you may need along the way.
